## Step-by-Step Installation Instructions

### Step 1: Install Your Player
1. Turn on your Samsung TV and press the **Home** button on your remote.
2. Navigate to **Apps (Samsung Smart Hub)**.
3. In the search bar, type **IBO Player** (or your preferred app) and click **Install**.

### Step 2: Retrieve Device Identifiers
1. Launch the installed app.
2. The welcome screen will display your **MAC Address** and a **Device Key**. Keep these handy.

### Step 3: Connect Your Subscription
1. You can upload your M3U link on the player's web portal, or simply **send your MAC and Device Key to our WhatsApp support team**.
2. We will immediately bind your high-speed 4K line to your application.

### Step 4: Refresh and Enjoy
1. Restart the app on your Samsung TV.
2. Your live categories, movies, series, and sports channels will be ready to watch in full Ultra HD!

## Performance Optimization Tips

* **Wired Connection**: For uninterrupted 4K streaming, use a direct Ethernet cable rather than 2.4GHz Wi-Fi.
* **Cache Management**: Go to **Settings > Support > Device Care > Manage Storage** on your TV and clear app caches if navigation feels sluggish.
