## The Root Cause of Sports Stream Freezing

Most sports streams freeze during major weekend matches due to three fundamental bottlenecks:
1. **Server Overload (Throttled Bandwidth):** Free and cheap providers host thousands of concurrent viewers on cheap shared VPS servers that collapse during peak moments.
2. **ISP Bandwidth Throttling:** Major internet service providers detect continuous high-bitrate video traffic and artificially choke your connection during match hours.
3. **Suboptimal Video Codecs:** Using outdated H.264 decoders instead of modern H.265 (HEVC) hardware decoding leads to stuttering on Smart TVs and Firesticks.

If you are using **TiviMate**, **IPTV Smarters Pro**, or **IBO Player**:
